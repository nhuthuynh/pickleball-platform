Pickleball Court & Social Play Platform

A marketplace connecting facility owners, hosts and players: book courts, configure pricing and discounts, run social games with automatic skill/gender matching, and promote competitions through linked social channels. One account, contextual roles.

Audience: Product & Design
Depth: High-level
Platforms: Web · iPad · iPhone
01

Roles & Permissions

Role is contextual, not a fixed account type. The same person can be a Player in one game, Host of another, and Owner of the facility both take place in.

Player
Books courts, finds and joins social games, brings guests, builds a level from play history.
Host
Rents courts to run social games/competitions, sets rules & payment method, advertises via social media.
Facility Owner
Lists the facility, configures court pricing & discounts, links cameras, can also host games directly.
Club
Organization account that hires courts on recurring days/times at owner-configured discount rates.

Domain Model

High-level entity clusters and how they connect — not a schema, a map of what the product needs to track.

Identity
Account — one profile, holds Player/Host/Owner capability per context
Player Level — derived from tenure & win record; feeds auto-matching
Facility & Courts
Facility — description, photos, address, camera links, owner
Court — belongs to a facility
Pricing Rule — date/time price, monthly price, discount period + end condition, per court
Games & Bookings
Booking — a court reserved for a date/time, by a player, host, or club
Social Game — rules (cancellation, capacity, payment method), matching config, sits on a booking
Participant — a player + guest count joined to a game
Payment — online or cash, tied to a booking/game
Growth
Competition — organized by a host, reserves courts across dates
Social Account Link — WhatsApp, Facebook Group, X, Instagram connected once per host

Payments

Host or owner sets the accepted method per game or booking: online only, cash only, or either — the player sees this before joining.

Online
Charged at booking or join time through the platform. Confirmed instantly, refunded automatically if cancelled within the host's free-cancel window.
Cash
Reservation held as pending, settled in person at the facility. Host marks it paid after the game; no platform payout involved.

Platform Notes — Web, iPad, iPhone

One shared design system and component set across platforms; layouts reflow by breakpoint rather than diverging.

Web · ≥1280px
Multi-column dashboards; calendar and list side-by-side; persistent sidebar nav for hosts/owners.
iPad · 768–1180px
Two-column list + detail where space allows; collapsible nav; touch targets ≥44px.
iPhone · <600px
Single-column stacked flows; bottom tab bar (Discover, Bookings, Games, Profile); full-screen modal forms.
10

Assumptions & Open Questions

· Camera integration assumes facilities connect an existing system (partner API or stream link) — not a platform-owned camera product.
· Social platforms may restrict automated reading of replies; some ad flows may need the host to confirm registrations manually.
· Payment processor for the "online" method is unspecified — assumed a standard card/wallet processor with platform payout to owners/hosts.
· Player level formula (tenure + win rate) needs a product decision on weighting before matching logic is finalized.
